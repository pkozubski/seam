Privacy policy

Effective date: 1 November 2026

In short

  • Seam has no account, ads, analytics SDKs or usage telemetry.
  • Keyboard, mouse, clipboard, audio and screen data travel only between your paired Mac and PC on your local network, encrypted.
  • The free version sends nothing over the internet except update checks to GitHub.
  • Seam Pro sends your license key and the device name to our license server (api.seamdesk.app) when you activate it and about once a week to validate it.

Who is responsible

The controller of personal data described here is Paweł Kozubski, Aleja Zdobywców Wału Pomorskiego 72E/21, 78-600 Wałcz, Poland, contact: support@seamdesk.app. Purchases are processed by Stripe as reseller and merchant of record (see “Purchases” below).

Data processed on your computers

When the corresponding features are on, Seam processes keyboard and mouse events, clipboard text or images, device names, local IP addresses, system audio and the picture of the Mac virtual display (only the virtual display, never the built-in or other physical Mac screens) or of the Windows virtual monitor. These data are transmitted only between the paired Windows computer and Mac on the local network, encrypted with AES-256-GCM. The application does not send their contents to us or to any cloud service.

The pairing secret is stored in the macOS Keychain or encrypted for the current Windows account with DPAPI. Preferences, the trial start date, the license key and recent diagnostic log entries are stored locally on each computer.

Update checks

If update checks are on (the default; you can turn them off in Settings), the application contacts the GitHub API and GitHub release hosting (github.com/pkozubski/seam-releases). GitHub receives ordinary connection metadata such as your public IP address and the user agent. No clipboard, input, audio or license data is included. See GitHub's privacy statement for its processing.

If you install the virtual monitor driver for Windows apps on the Mac, the Windows application downloads the Virtual Display Driver and nefcon releases from GitHub once, verifies their SHA-256 checksums and installs them with administrator approval.

Seam Pro licensing

The trial needs no account or payment details. Its start date is stored only on your computer.

When you activate Seam Pro, the application sends to our license server (api.seamdesk.app, hosted on Cloudflare Workers):

  • on activation: the license key and the device name (the computer name set in macOS or Windows);
  • on validation, about once every 7 days while the computer is online: the license key and the activation ID returned at activation;
  • on deactivation, when you choose Deactivate: the license key and the activation ID.

Cloudflare also receives your public IP address with each request (see “This website”); the license server does not store it. The server replies with the license status and the email address of the purchase, which the application shows in Settings → License and stores locally. Nothing else, and none of the data from the previous sections, is sent. If validation is not possible, Pro keeps working for up to 30 days since the last successful check.

For each license the server stores the key, the purchase email address and country, the Stripe order reference, and, for each activated computer, its device name, activation ID and the time of the last check. Deactivating a computer deletes its entry.

We send your license key by email after a purchase and when you ask for it again on seamdesk.app/license. These emails go through Resend (resend.com), which processes your email address and the message only to deliver it.

Purchases

Seam Pro is sold through Stripe Managed Payments: Stripe acts as reseller and merchant of record. Stripe collects the details needed for payment, tax and fraud prevention (for example your name, email, billing country and payment method) under its own privacy policy: stripe.com/privacy. We receive order information from Stripe, such as your name, email address, country and order details, to issue the license and provide support and refunds. We process this to perform the contract and to meet tax obligations, and keep it as long as your order exists in our Stripe account and at least as long as tax law requires (usually 5 years).

This website

  • The site is hosted on Cloudflare. Cloudflare processes connection data such as your IP address and user agent to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. These logs are kept only briefly for security. The site sets no cookies and uses no analytics.
  • The site sets no cookies and loads no third-party fonts or analytics. Fonts are served from this domain.
  • Your browser's local storage remembers only your language choice (English or Polish) so the site stops suggesting the other version.
  • “Buy Pro” is a plain link to Stripe’s checkout page; this site loads no Stripe script. The license page contacts api.seamdesk.app only to show your key after a purchase or to send it again.
  • Download buttons link to files hosted on GitHub.

Diagnostics

Logs remain on your device unless you choose to copy and send them to us. Please remove device names, IP addresses and other personal information before sharing a diagnostic report.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under the EU General Data Protection Regulation, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (in Poland: the President of the Personal Data Protection Office, UODO). To exercise these rights, write to support@seamdesk.app. Requests about payment data can also be sent to Stripe.

We process the license key and device name to provide the license you bought (performance of a contract) and website connection data to keep the site secure (legitimate interest). Order data stay in our Stripe account and license data on our license server (Cloudflare); the license key and its status are also stored on your own computer. GitHub, Cloudflare, Stripe and Resend may process data outside the European Economic Area under the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

Changes

We will update this policy before adding telemetry, accounts or any other external service, and show the effective date at the top.
