Activate Pro

  1. Open Seam on your Mac or PC and go to Settings → License.
  2. Paste the key and click Activate.
  3. One key works on up to 3 computers, and one licensed computer in a Mac–PC pair unlocks Pro for both.

Lost your key?

Enter the email address you used at checkout and we’ll send your keys again.

New computer?

On the old one, open Settings → License and click Deactivate to free its slot. Can’t reach it anymore? Write to support@seamdesk.app and we’ll reset your devices.
