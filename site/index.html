Security and privacy

Your data never leaves home.

Seam works only on your local network. No servers, no account, no cloud. You pair the computers with a one-time code, and the secret goes into the Keychain on the Mac and DPAPI on Windows. Control, clipboard and audio are encrypted and protected against replay.

0

No ads, analytics or telemetry.

Seam goes online only to check for updates and, if you want, to download the virtual monitor driver. With Pro, it also checks your license key with Lemon Squeezy about once a week. Logs stay on your computer.
